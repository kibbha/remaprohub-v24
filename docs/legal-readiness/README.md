# ReMaPro Hub — legal readiness status

This separates engineering work that can be completed now from items that depend on the final operator, launch configuration or professional legal review.

## Implemented now
- proprietary ReMaPro licence notice;
- direct third-party component register;
- manifest-level CycloneDX SBOM;
- asset provenance register;
- contributor/IP provenance rules;
- acquisition/infrastructure transfer runbook;
- existing in-app privacy and account-deletion pages;
- IP consistency check and release gate;
- repository-separation plan.

## Pending before commercial launch

### Legal operator / ownership
- choose whether the rights-holder remains an individual or becomes a company;
- if a company becomes owner, execute written assignment of the relevant IP/domain/brand rights;
- put production vendor accounts under the final business-controlled identity.

### Trade mark
- perform a proper clearance search for ReMaPro / ReMaPro Hub / ReMaPro POS, including similar signs;
- decide classes/territories and file if appropriate.

### Privacy / data protection
The current privacy page is a product draft. Before launch validate it against actual production data flows and add, where required:
- actual controller/legal operator and usable contact details;
- purposes/categories of processing;
- recipients/subprocessors;
- retention/deletion rules;
- cross-border transfers and safeguards;
- data-subject rights and request route;
- incident/data-breach procedure;
- final analytics, AI, billing, hosting and support providers.

### Commercial documents
- customer/SaaS terms;
- subscription/cancellation/refund terms aligned with store/billing channels;
- DPA/data-processing terms for business customers where required;
- acceptable-use/support/SLA positions if offered;
- current subprocessor/vendor register.

### Store publication
- final Google Play privacy/data-safety answers;
- final support contact and public privacy-policy URL;
- account-deletion workflow verification.

## Rule
Do not mark these items complete based on assumptions. Complete them only against the real launch entity, providers, countries, pricing and data flows.
